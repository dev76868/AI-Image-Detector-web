PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your photo is for the scan—not for a permanent archive.

Real Or Fake uses Apple’s system photo picker so the app works with the image you select rather than scanning your whole library. When server analysis is required, the selected image is sent over encrypted HTTPS to the app’s analysis infrastructure.
